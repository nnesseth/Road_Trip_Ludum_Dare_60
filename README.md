You are embarking on a road trip! Jump in the car and drive.

CHARACTER MOVEMENT CONTROLS

Use the mouse to look around, WASD to move your character, press E to enter the car, and then drive the car!

CAR DRIVING CONTROLS

To start driving, shift the car from Park to Drive by pressing R to cycle through Park, Reverse, Neutral, Drive.

R to upshift

Q to downshift

W to hit the gas

S to hit the brakes

See if you can make it to the end! We would love to hear what you think of the driving experience so far! We had many more ideas and features we are excited to add to this game, but this is the farthest we could get in the jam’s time frame.

We plan to continue future development and we will release a more polished demo by the end of the month! Stay tuned if you want to see more.
